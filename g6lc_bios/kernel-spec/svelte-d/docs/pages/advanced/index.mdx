# Advanced

The development guide assumes the compiler is present and the IR is the one you meant. This section is what happens when that assumption is the thing you are debugging. [LDC 1.43 setup](./platform) is the single compiler on every OS, and why a 1.42 `ldc2` on `PATH` is not a fallback. [wasm-eh](./wasm-eh) is why `try/catch` works and why `.await` does not. [Incremental compile](./incremental) is reprint-skip, opposite-cell-skip, and per-`.o`. [Debug map](./debug) is how a wasm stack frame becomes a `.svelte` line.

None of this changes the IR contract. It is how the contract stays cheap to re-check.
