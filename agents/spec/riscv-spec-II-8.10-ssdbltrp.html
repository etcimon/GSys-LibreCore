Vol II §8.10 — Ssdbltrp Double Trap Extension

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#ssdbltrp. Source line: specs/riscv-spec.html:89078. Domain: SPEC.

Ssdbltrp is the supervisor-mode counterpart of Smdbltrp, defining double-trap behavior in S-mode and the associated delegation and reporting. It ensures that a trap taken while in S-mode trap handler is handled safely rather than corrupting trap state.

Spec quotation

"The Ssdbltrp extension addresses a double trap privilege modes lower
than M. It enables HS-mode to invoke a critical error handler in a
virtual machine on a double trap in VS-mode. It also allows M-mode to
invoke a critical error handler in the OS/Hypervisor on a double trap in
S/HS-mode."
...
"The Ssdbltrp extension adds the menvcfg.DTE and the sstatus.SDT fields."
(specs/riscv-spec.html:89078-89085, anchor #ssdbltrp)

CVA6 status: absent. There is no double-trap state machine in core/controller.sv and no menvcfg.DTE or sstatus.SDT fields in core/csr_regfile.sv. A trap taken during S-mode trap handling would overwrite sepc/scause/stval in the normal CSR update path.

.dts linkage: no DT node; Ssdbltrp would be advertised through the riscv,isa string and the menvcfg/sstatus field descriptions if implemented.
