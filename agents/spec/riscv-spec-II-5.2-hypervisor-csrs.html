Vol II §5.2 — Hypervisor and Virtual Supervisor CSRs

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#_hypervisor_and_virtual_supervisor_csrs. Source line: specs/riscv-spec.html:79917. Domain: —.

This subchapter defines the hypervisor CSRs (hstatus, hedeleg, hideleg, hcounteren, etc.) and the virtual supervisor CSRs (vsstatus, vsie, vsip, vstvec, vsscratch, vsepc, vscause, vstval, vsatp). They are the S-mode CSRs shadowed for guest context and are essential for two-stage translation and virtual interrupts.

Spec quotation

"An OS or hypervisor running in HS-mode uses the supervisor CSRs to
interact with the exception, interrupt, and address-translation
subsystems. Additional CSRs are provided to HS-mode, but not to VS-mode,
to manage two-stage address translation and to control the behavior of a
VS-mode guest: hstatus, hedeleg, hideleg, hvip, hip, hie, hgeip, hgeie,
henvcfg, henvcfgh, hcounteren, htimedelta, htimedeltah, htval, htinst,
and hgatp."
(specs/riscv-spec.html:79917-79924, anchor #_hypervisor_and_virtual_supervisor_csrs)
