Vol I chapter 10 — Zpm Packed SIMD (P Extension)

Canonical: #zp (10. Packed SIMD Extensions). Source line: specs/riscv-spec.html:42380.

The P extension (Zpm) defines packed SIMD operations on subword integer data within the XLEN registers. It targets DSP and low-cost signal-processing without a full vector unit. It adds packed add/sub, multiply, shift, compare, and clip operations.

Spec quotation

"This chapter is a placeholder for the forthcoming P and Zp* extensions
for packed SIMD within the x registers. It is included so that chapter
numbering will not change once the extensions are ratified and
incorporated."
(specs/riscv-spec.html:42380-42394, anchor #zp)

CVA6 status: absent; no P-extension logic in the integer ALU. The core/decoder.sv does not decode packed-SIMD opcodes and core/alu.sv has no packed-lane data path.

.dts linkage: no DT node; the P extension is not ratified and is not advertised in the riscv,isa string.
