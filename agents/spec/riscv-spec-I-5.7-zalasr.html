Vol I §5.7 — Zalasr Extension for Atomic Load-Acquire and Store-Release Instructions

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#ext:zalasr. Domain: SPEC.

Zalasr exposes standalone load-acquire and store-release semantics without requiring the full AMO encoding. The Zalasr extension provides load-acquire and store-release instructions in RISC-V. These can be important for high-performance designs by enabling finer-grained synchronisation than is possible with fences alone, by providing a unidirectional fence. The Zalasr instructions are atomic loads and stores that support ordering annotations.

CVA6 status: absent. The A extension supports LR/SC acquire/release bits (core/decoder.sv:1602-1637, core/load_store_unit.sv:728-748), but there are no standalone load-acquire / store-release instruction encodings decoded in core/decoder.sv. The LSU/store unit does not generate ordering annotations for ordinary loads/stores beyond the existing amo_t operations. Adding Zalasr would require new decode slots, a way to carry acquire/release ordering bits through the LSU/Store Buffer, and memory-system checks that enforce the unidirectional fence relative to ordinary loads/stores.

.dts linkage: no DT node; Zalasr capability would be reported through riscv,isa and would require the memory@ PMA nodes to be cacheable/coherent, as with other atomic extensions.
