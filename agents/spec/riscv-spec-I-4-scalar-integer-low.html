Vol I §4.2-4.16 — Scalar Integer Extensions (Zicsr, Zicntr, M, Zmmul, Zicond, ...)

Canonical anchors: #ext:zicsr (4.2-4.3 Zicsr), #ext:m (4.5 M/Zmmul), #ext:zicond (4.16 Zicond), #ext:zicntr/#ext:zihpm (4.12-4.13 counters). Source lines: specs/riscv-spec.html:9064 (Zicsr), :9603 (M), :10063 (Zicond), :10627 (Zicntr/Zihpm).

This group contains the remaining unprivileged scalar integer extensions: Zicsr (CSRRW/CSRRS/CSRRC and their immediate/rd=x0 variants), M/Zmmul (multiply/divide instructions), Zicntr/Zihpm (cycle, instret, and hpmcounters), and Zicond (conditional move and zero). They are pure ISA decode and execute additions that map to the CSR file, the integer multiplier/divider, and the ALU.

CVA6 status: implemented. Zicsr instructions are decoded in core/decoder.sv and executed in core/csr_regfile.sv; the CSR address is in instr[31:20]. M/Zmmul operations are handled by core/mult.sv and core/multiplier.sv, routed through the issue stage. Zicond operations are decoded and executed in core/alu.sv when CVA6Cfg.RVZiCond is set. Counters (cycle, instret, hpmcounters) are maintained in core/perf_counters.sv and the CSR file.

.dts linkage: these extensions appear in the riscv,isa string as _zicsr, _m/_zmmul, _zicond, _zicntr, and _zihpm* where applicable.
