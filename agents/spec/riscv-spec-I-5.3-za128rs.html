Vol I §5.3 — Za128rs Extension for Reservation-Set Size

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#ext:za128rs. Domain: RAM.

Za128rs declares that the implementation guarantees a maximum reservation-set size of 128 bytes for LR/SC. The Za128rs extension requires that the reservation sets used by the instructions in the Zalrsc extension be contiguous, naturally aligned, and at most 128 bytes in size.

Spec quotation

"The Za128rs extension requires that the reservation sets used by the
instructions in the Zalrsc extension be contiguous, naturally aligned,
and at most 128 bytes in size."
(specs/riscv-spec.html:14742-14745, anchor #ext:za128rs / #norm:za128rs_res_set_req)

CVA6 status: not guaranteed. As with Za64rs, CVA6 does not implement a 128-byte reservation-set guarantee. The cache-line widths in shipped targets are 16 or 32 bytes (core/include/config_pkg.sv), far below 128 bytes. The LR/SC reservation monitoring in core/cache_subsystem/hpdcache/rtl/src/hpdcache_ctrl.sv:157-161,1179-1181 and core/cache_subsystem/wt_dcache_missunit.sv operates at cache-line or word granularity. Za128rs would require a wider reservation register / snoop mask and a 128-byte cache-line or a software-visible reservation-set size, neither of which exists.

.dts linkage: no DT node; Za128rs would be exposed through riscv,isa only when the reservation-set size is raised to at least 128 bytes and verified.
