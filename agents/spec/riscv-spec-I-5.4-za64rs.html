Vol I §5.4 — Za64rs Extension for Reservation-Set Size

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#ext:za64rs. Domain: RAM.

Za64rs guarantees a maximum LR/SC reservation-set size of 64 bytes. The Za64rs extension requires that the reservation sets used by the instructions in the Zalrsc extension be contiguous, naturally aligned, and at most 64 bytes in size. This is the common case when combined with Zic64b (64-byte cache blocks), because the reservation is naturally bounded by the cache-line size.

CVA6 status: implemented when CVA6Cfg.RVZa64rs is set. corev_apu/src/g6lc_axi_lrsc.sv keys the reservation table on exact AXI address (a 1-address set: contiguous, aligned, at most 64 bytes). Product D$ lines are 16 B (DcacheLineWidth 128), so invalidation cannot grow the set past 64 B. core/g6lc_za64rs.sv is the membership leaf and advertise predicate (RVA and DCACHE_LINE_WIDTH <= 512). Isolated tb_g6lc_za64rs.sv. Do not 64-byte-mask the monitor (adjacent locks must stay independent). Za128rs and Zic64b stay unadvertised.

.dts linkage: advertise za64rs in riscv,isa-extensions on packages that set RVZa64rs (g6lc64_ooo_int2_l3, _v_ai, ooo_server, smt2).
