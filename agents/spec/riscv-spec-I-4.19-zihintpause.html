Vol I §4.19 — Zihintpause Extension for Pause Hint

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#ext:zihintpause. Domain: SPEC.

PAUSE is a HINT instruction that indicates the current hart's rate of instruction retirement should be temporarily reduced or paused; the duration of its effect must be bounded and may be zero. It is normally used to save energy and execution resources while polling. PAUSE is encoded as a FENCE instruction with pred=W, succ=0, fm=0, rd=x0, and rs1=x0.

CVA6 status: implemented (config) — U7ᵃ. When CVA6Cfg.ZihintpauseEn is set, core/decoder.sv recognises the PAUSE HINT encoding (OpcodeMiscMem, funct3=0, imm==12'h010, rd=rs1=x0) and decodes it as ALU ADD (NOP) so it does not take the full D$ fence path. With the bit clear, PAUSE still falls through as FENCE (architecturally legal HINT, suboptimal for spin loops). Primary Linux target enables the bit. No separate micro-op or fetch throttle; energy gating remains a U8ᵇ item.

.dts linkage: no DT node; advertise via riscv,isa / riscv,isa-extensions (zihintpause) when enabled.
