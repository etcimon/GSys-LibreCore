Vol II §5.4 — Machine-Level CSRs

Canonical: specs/riscv-spec.html#_machine_level_csrs_2. Source line: specs/riscv-spec.html:81791. Domain: —.

This subchapter extends M-mode CSRs for hypervisor support: mstatus bits MPV and MPP modifications, medeleg/mideleg hypervisor bits, and the second-stage translation root hgatp. These allow M-mode to delegate traps to HS-mode and configure the host stage-2 page table.

Spec quotation

"The hypervisor extension augments or modifies machine CSRs mstatus,
mstatush, mideleg, mip, and mie, and adds CSRs mtval2 and mtinst."
(specs/riscv-spec.html:81791-81794, anchor #_machine_level_csrs_2)

CVA6 status: absent. The mstatus/mideleg/mip/mie CSRs in core/csr_regfile.sv do not include MPV/GVA/MTVAL2/MTINST fields, and hgatp is not implemented. Trap delegation in core/controller.sv handles only M/S/U.

.dts linkage: no hypervisor DT node; h is not present in the riscv,isa string.
