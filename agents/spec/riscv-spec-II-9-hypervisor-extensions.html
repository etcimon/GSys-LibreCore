Vol II chapter 9 — Sh Hypervisor Extensions

Canonical anchors: #shvstvecd (9.1), #shcounterenw (9.2), #shvstvala (9.3), #shtvala (9.4), #shvsatpa (9.5), #shgatpa (9.6), #sha (9.7). Source line: specs/riscv-spec.html:89115 (Shvstvecd).

The Sh extensions are hypervisor-tuning add-ons: direct trap vectoring (Shvstvecd), counter-enable writability (Shcounterenw), trap value reporting for VS and HS modes (Shvstvala, Shtvala), translation-mode support for VS-mode and guest-stage satp (Shvsatpa, Shgatpa), and the augmented hypervisor extension (Sha). They all depend on the H extension being present and refine hypervisor behavior rather than introducing major new mechanisms.

Spec quotation

"If the Shvstvecd extension is implemented, then vstvec.MODE must be
capable of holding the value 0 (Direct). Furthermore, when
vstvec.MODE=Direct, vstvec.BASE must be capable of holding any valid
four-byte-aligned address."
(specs/riscv-spec.html:89115-89120, anchor #shvstvecd)

CVA6 status: absent. The H extension is not implemented in CVA6 (CVA6Cfg.RVH is not enabled in shipped targets), so the VS/HS-mode CSRs and hypervisor-specific trap/translation logic in core/csr_regfile.sv, core/cva6_mmu/, and core/controller.sv are absent. All Sh extensions are therefore absent.

.dts linkage: no hypervisor DT node; h is not present in the riscv,isa string.
